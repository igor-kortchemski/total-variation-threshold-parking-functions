# Provenance

The mathematical source of record is Igor Kortchemski's *[A total variation threshold for the first coordinates of uniform parking functions via Lorentzian polynomials](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40082v1)*, arXiv:2610.40082v1, submitted 29 October 2026. The [source comparison](../evidence/arxiv-v1-correspondence.json) records the public source archive and TeX hashes and confirms that all 15 audited theorem, lemma, proposition, remark and proof environments are byte-identical. The mathematical body after the introduction is unchanged; differences concern introductory wording, acknowledgments and bibliography presentation. The article is distributed under the [arXiv non-exclusive distribution license](https://arxiv.org/licenses/nonexclusive-distrib/1.0/); neither its source nor PDF is redistributed here.

The original formalization manuscript has SHA-256 `7670d0719c75a0c860181730b7fdffb39f96ca88d2d6f185daefc29ec9ad839b`, and its bibliography has SHA-256 `b89cef4b5dd22db1861271ad2d3b6d2e041ffc8d97877275e2fa115e888f35e0`. The later manuscript audited on 29 October has SHA-256 `801831ad26108af44e6ffeff29e236e19f712e26da0ede2d51daffd45482adf5`; its unchanged mathematical environments were compared with both the earlier source and the arXiv source. These historical hashes remain evidence of the reviewed inputs, not identifiers of the public v1 archive.

The reconstructed library starts from development revision `c8a3de82a5b353cd2216040717e53b49fa1f8ce9` and includes subsequent proof reconstruction. That ancestral commit alone does not identify the reconstructed snapshot: [the historical source manifest](../evidence/history-4.34/final-source-hashes.json) identifies its production source by content. The export's reader-comment changes have separate historical evidence. [License-header evidence](../evidence/license-header-update.json) records the Apache-2.0 step and preservation of every byte after each initial comment at that step. Six later proof-only edits across four files are recorded in [structural adaptations](../evidence/structural-proof-adaptations.json) and [compatibility repairs](../evidence/toolchain-proof-repairs.json). The [independent adaptation review](../evidence/active-source-review.md) accounts for every noncomment change and confirms unchanged statement and data-bearing definition source. The [active source manifest](../evidence/source-manifest.json) identifies the current export.

An earlier parallel AI-generated formalization, OpenMathModelFiles, supplied the proof of `sequence_lemma`, the exchangeability and coordinate-law definitions, the coordinate-permutation exchangeability proof, and the Borel-weight definition and elementary identity. These appear in `Parking.SequenceLemma`, `Parking.Exchangeable`, and `Parking.Borel`. The original provenance credits an independent auditor for the coordinate-permutation proof and the Borel identity, without identifying a separate human author. The reconstructed library proves its cited mathematical interfaces; the earlier ledger's unfinished proofs and axioms are not imported or redistributed. No publicly accessible repository for that ledger is asserted. The source text hashes are `ec1f562a2c32e3d107384003530b1dd91272f7c4c40ea9da7f2f54f0d32ee0e7` for the sequence proof and `861e00b97b9ea305dc6ad633a04c7ea8e524d71ca2a14640cdcf4d0c43fec4fe` for the ledger.

The analytic Hessian implication in `Parking.Paper.StrongLogConcavity` is adapted from `ParkingThreshold.ParkingLogConcavity` in a parallel formalization of the same manuscript. That source has SHA-256 `8884e428ec148077c8921a3b9e909e817090906fed53f7a80534ff09fe930fa1`. The adaptation is attributed in the module comment; the multiset-of-derivatives bridge and subsequent reconstruction are proved in this library.

The author directed the work and supplied the mathematical manuscript. Codex and parallel AI agents developed proofs, inspected actual proof-value dependencies, and cross-reviewed source correspondence. The preparation does not claim independent human peer review. Existing complete Lean proofs of exact cited specializations were reused where they support the manuscript's argument; no proof axiom substitutes for a citation.

On 29 October 2026 the user approved the concrete export-only proposal with “Approuve”, including the Apache-2.0 license and the described parallel-ledger redistribution scope. The root LICENSE applies to this export; author/year notices and the preceding attribution are retained. Those initial approvals were followed by the author's explicit request to make the repository public on 29 October, then the 30 October approval of arXiv metadata, correspondence checking, final verification and v1.0.1/Palomar submission preparation.
